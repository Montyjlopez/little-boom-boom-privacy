Little Boom Boom — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 24, 2026

Little Boom Boom is an app for young children made by Monty Lopez. We take children's privacy seriously.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app does use the internet for

The app downloads episode pictures (thumbnails, some from YouTube's image server) so it can show them on the Watch screen. These requests do not include any information about you or your child.

Links to YouTube

Tapping an episode shows a grown-up check (a math question). Only after it is answered does the app open YouTube, which has its own privacy policy. We do not send any information to YouTube.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because the app collects no personal information, no data from children is collected, used, or disclosed.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
